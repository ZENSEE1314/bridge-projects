02 · Why now

"Nobar" is a national habit. Nobody has premiumised it.

  • Watching together is the culture. "Nonton bareng" (nobar) is how Indonesians socialise — but at home it means a phone screen and a crowded family sofa.
  • Homes lack privacy & comfort. Couples, students and friends have nowhere private, cool and comfortable to watch a series uninterrupted.
  • The game-room model has a ceiling. PS5/Xbox rooms at Rp50k/hr compete only on price, skew male, and can't upsell.
  • Loneliness is a real, spendable need. Rental-companion services thrive in Japan, Korea & China. It has never been offered here, packaged safely.

Batam blends low Indonesian operating costs with a young, connected, weekend-tourism population and strong Singaporean visitor flow — ideal for a novel leisure concept that needs footfall but wants cheap rent and labour.

03 · Two ways every room earns

Room revenue keeps the lights on. Companions make it exceptional.

Stream 1 · The Room
Rp50k/hr
From a 1-hour booking (Rp50k entry). 100% ours, open 24/7. Priced at the market floor to drive high volume.
Stream 2 · The Companion
Rp500k/4hr flat
Split 50/50 — Rp250k net to chillX per block, near-zero fixed cost. A flat fee, so guests naturally book longer rooms to use it fully.
Stream 3 · F&B
~60% margin
Snacks, drinks & light bites ordered to the room. Optional but lifts average spend and dwell time.

The flat-companion trick: because the companion is one flat Rp500k for up to 4 hours, a guest who books one is motivated to extend their room toward the full 4 hours to get their money's worth — lifting room-hours per session and overall utilisation. Companions are independent contractors paid entirely from their 50% share, so the line carries no fixed payroll risk.

10 · Trust, safety & compliance

The companion service is platonic, professional, and protected — by design.

This is the make-or-break of the concept, and we treat it as a first-class part of the operating model, not an afterthought. chillX's companion offering is companionship only: conversation and shared viewing, nothing else.

GUEST & BRAND SAFETY
  • Strictly 18+, ID-verified for every companion and, where relevant, every guest.
  • Vetted, trained, contracted companions with a written code of conduct and a clear "companionship, not intimacy" boundary.
  • CCTV in all common areas & corridors; glass-panel doors; a panic button and staff presence on every floor.
  • Zero-tolerance policy — either party can end a session instantly; misconduct means removal.
LEGAL & LICENSING TO CONFIRM
  • Local permits: entertainment / hospitality business licences and zoning to be secured before opening.
  • Streaming rights: position as private personal viewing (guests use their own accounts) and pursue proper commercial/public-viewing licensing where required.
  • Labour & tax: companions engaged as compliant independent contractors with clear terms.
  • Insurance: public liability and premises cover from day one.

We flag the right-hand column openly: these are the diligence items an investor should expect us to close during fit-out. Getting them right is a moat — it makes the concept defensible and hard to copy cheaply.

11 · Go-to-market & moat

Launch fast on social. Win on experience.

How we fill the rooms

  • TikTok & Instagram first. The rooms are inherently photogenic — every guest is a content creator marketing us for free.
  • Couples, students, groups, tourists. Date nights, birthdays, K-drama marathons, match screenings, Singaporean weekenders.
  • Booking app + memberships for repeat visits, off-peak & overnight pricing to fill the quiet hours, and referral credits.
  • Signature companions build personal followings that pull their own bookings.

Why it's hard to copy

  • First mover in a category that doesn't yet exist in Batam.
  • The safe, trusted companion operation is operationally hard — reputation compounds and can't be bought overnight.
  • Owned building = structural cost advantage no rent-paying copycat can match.
  • Brand & content flywheel — the more it's shared, the cheaper acquisition gets.
